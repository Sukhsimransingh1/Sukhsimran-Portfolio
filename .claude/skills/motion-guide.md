# Motion Guide

Motion exists to communicate.

Not entertain.

---

Allowed animations:

Fade

Slide

Reveal

Scale

Blur reveal

Stagger

Number counting

Page transitions

---

Avoid:

Particles

Floating icons

Random parallax

Continuous movement

Long looping animations

Distracting effects

---

Animation Rules

200–600ms duration.

Ease-in-out.

Consistent timing.

Subtle.

Elegant.

Support prefers-reduced-motion.

Never animate everything simultaneously.

Motion should improve readability.
